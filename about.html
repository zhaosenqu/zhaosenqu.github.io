Biography

I am an undergraduate in Electrical Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, with a minor in Mathematics, expecting to graduate in May 2027. My research spans the path from material to device: thin-film deposition and synthesis, nanofabrication, structural characterization, and electrical measurement.

As an NSF Singh REU intern in Prof. Deep Jariwala’s group at the University of Pennsylvania, I scaled aluminum scandium nitride (AlScN) ferroelectric diode memory toward the 40 nm node and studied proximity ferroelectricity in AlN/AlScN stacks. As a DOE ORISE intern with Dr. Kyle Kelley at Oak Ridge National Laboratory’s Center for Nanophase Materials Sciences, I optimized nitrided interlayers for low-temperature reactively sputtered AlN films. In Prof. Timothy Weihs’s group at Johns Hopkins, I studied structure–property relationships in sputter-deposited Cu, Cu–Ti, and Ti–V alloys.

I now work with Prof. Thomas Kempa in the Department of Chemistry at Johns Hopkins on metal-organic framework / transition-metal dichalcogenide heterostructures. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and intend to pursue a research career in semiconductor devices and materials.
