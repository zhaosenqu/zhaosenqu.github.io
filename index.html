Zhaosen Qu

Undergraduate researcher in electrical engineering at Johns Hopkins University, working on ferroelectric memory, nanoscale semiconductor fabrication, and thin-film process integration.

My research follows electronic materials from synthesis and deposition through device fabrication and electrical testing. I have scaled AlScN ferroelectric diode memory toward the 40 nm node at the University of Pennsylvania, developed low-temperature sputtered AlN films at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and now study metal-organic framework / 2D semiconductor heterostructures in the Kempa Group at Johns Hopkins.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and intend to pursue a research career in semiconductor devices and materials.
