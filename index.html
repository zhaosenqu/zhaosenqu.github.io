02 / Research

Three problems I have worked on

A

Current · Kempa Group, Johns Hopkins

Can a programmable framework reshape the electronic landscape of a 2D semiconductor?

I synthesize bulk and few-layer metal-organic frameworks by chemical vapor deposition, transfer them onto monolayer transition-metal dichalcogenides, and examine heterostructure morphology and optical response.

CVD · layer transfer · AFM · optical spectroscopy

B

2025 · Singh REU, University of Pennsylvania

What limits ferroelectric memory when the device is scaled to tens of nanometers?

I fabricated AlScN ferroelectric diodes down to the 40 nm node and measured their switching, leakage, and reliability. Contact engineering lowered the coercive field by 20% and leakage current by 10×; the work contributed to a Nano Letters article.

E-beam lithography · PVD · RIE · lift-off · P–E and I–V measurement

C

2026 · DOE ORISE, Oak Ridge National Laboratory

How can crystalline AlN be integrated within a lower thermal budget?

I studied nitrided interlayers for reactively sputtered AlN on plasma-treated SiO2. Sapphire controls and structural measurements helped separate substrate and process effects in a 350 °C route intended for photonic integration.

Reactive sputtering · XRD · AFM · Raman spectroscopy
